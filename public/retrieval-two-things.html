Two things

A tiny retrieval pause that everyone can complete.

How to run it

  1. Choose the focus

    Identify a taught idea from an earlier lesson.

  2. Recall two

    Each student independently records two accurate details without notes.

  3. Share one

    Partners explain one detail each, without simply copying.

  4. Verify

    Sample responses, clarify errors and ask for a corrected statement.

Check learning

A question about what students want to learn is reflection, not retrieval. Ask for knowledge they have already encountered.
