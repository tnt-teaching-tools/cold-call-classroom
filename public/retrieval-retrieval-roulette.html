Retrieval Roulette

Use a reusable bank to revisit older learning routinely.

How to run it

  1. Build your bank

    Use your own taught material and keep checked answers.

  2. Choose the range

    Limit selection to content already taught, including older learning.

  3. Retrieve together

    Display a short selection; everyone attempts before feedback.

  4. Respond to gaps

    Review answers, reteach a weak idea and revisit it later.

Check learning

Inspect the selected prompts before using them. This page explains the approach; it does not generate questions.
