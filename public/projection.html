How to project

Keep names, timers and pupil prompts on the classroom screen, with response records and teacher controls on your own device. Choose the setup that suits your classroom.

Laptop + extended display

Control from your laptop while pupils see a separate browser window.

  1. Connect your laptop to the classroom screen. On Windows press Windows + P → Extend. On Mac open System Settings → Displays, select the classroom screen and choose Use as → Extended display.
  2. Open Classroom display in a new browser window. Keep it private during pairing, then click Create pairing code.
  3. Click Copy link instead. Open another browser window on your laptop and paste the private link into its address bar. This window becomes your teacher workspace.
  4. In the teacher window click Connect this device. Compare the six-digit codes, then click Yes, connect this device in the display window.
  5. Drag only the Classroom display window onto the classroom screen and click Fullscreen. Keep the teacher window on your laptop.
  6. Use the normal classroom controls on your laptop. Record responses there; pupils see the separate display update.

If both screens show your teacher controls, you are duplicating or mirroring. Switch to Extend, or use the phone option.

Phone + mirrored laptop

Leave the laptop on the pupil display and control privately from your phone.

  1. On the laptop open Classroom display. Pair before showing it to pupils: keep the QR code and link private.
  2. Click Create pairing code, scan the QR code with your phone camera and open the link in Chrome or Safari.
  3. On your phone click Connect this device. Compare the six-digit code on both devices, then click Yes, connect this device on the laptop.
  4. Connect or mirror the laptop to the classroom screen. Click Fullscreen on the laptop and leave it on this page.
  5. Choose your class and run the lesson from your phone. Keep the phone awake and the page open. Names, timers and pupil prompts appear on the laptop; response records stay on the phone.
  6. When finished, click End session on the laptop or Disconnect screen on your phone.

Both devices need internet, but they do not need the same Wi-Fi. Your phone can use mobile data.

Before your lesson: Try a demonstration class to check what pupils can see. Both setups use an internet connection and sessions last up to two hours. Phone and laptop class lists save separately; use Settings → Export backup / Import backup to transfer classes to the device you will control from.

What about the ◫ Project button? It changes the current window into a simple pupil view. Exiting it returns to teacher controls in that same window, so those controls will also show on a mirrored screen. Use one of the setups above for private control. “Everyone responds” means pupils answer using your chosen method, such as mini whiteboards or paper; there is no student login or response app.
