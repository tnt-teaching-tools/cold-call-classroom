Privacy & data.

This notice explains how the Cold Call Classroom website handles class information, feedback and website visits.

01

Your class information

Class names, student first names and surname initials, attendance, participation history, response notes, rounds and settings are saved in this browser’s local storage. Students do not need accounts.

The app does not send these saved records to TNT Teaching Tools, GitHub or an analytics service. First names and initials can still identify students within a class; they are not automatically anonymous.

Use the tool in line with your school’s data policies. Avoid sensitive student details in notes. Use a protected device and browser profile: someone with access to the same profile may be able to see the saved information.

02

Keeping, deleting and transferring data

Local records remain until you remove them, clear the site’s browser storage or the browser removes that storage. In the app, use Settings → Delete all local data to remove saved class data from that browser. You can also delete individual classes or clear a class’s history.

Export backup saves a readable JSON file containing class information. It is not encrypted by the app. Keep it private and transfer it only through a method approved by your school.

Import backup reads a file locally and replaces the data in the current browser. Deleting browser data does not delete exported files or copies on another device. Delete those separately when no longer needed. TNT Teaching Tools cannot recover your local records.

03

Feedback you choose to send

TNT Teaching Tools receives your feedback type and message, plus your name and email if you choose to provide them. These details are used to respond to feedback, investigate problems and improve the tool.

Feedback is voluntary and is not required to use the app. Name and email are optional; without an email address, we cannot reply directly. Please do not include student names, class lists or sensitive information in your message.

The form sends the information you enter to FormSubmit, which forwards it to tntteachingandlearning@gmail.com, a Gmail inbox operated by TNT Teaching Tools. Saved class records are not attached.

We retain feedback for support and improvement, and remove personal details when no longer needed for those purposes or any applicable legal requirement. FormSubmit and the email provider may process information and technical logs under their own policies, including outside New Zealand.

04

Website hosting and external links

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visit information, such as IP address, browser or device information and request logs, to deliver and operate its services. This is separate from the class data saved by the app.

When you follow a further-reading or other external link, that website’s privacy policy applies. Linking to a source does not give it access to your saved class lists.

GitHub privacy statement

05

Analytics and browser storage

Google Analytics is optional. It loads only after you choose Allow analytics. Choose No thanks to use the app without it. Your choice is saved in this browser; use Analytics preferences at the top of the page to change it. Declining stops future analytics collection from this site; it does not remove information already received by Google.

When allowed, Google Analytics uses cookies and browser/device information to measure visits and general usage. We send a page visit and fixed events for opening the classroom, opening feedback and successfully submitting feedback. We do not send student names, class lists, response notes, feedback text or email addresses to analytics.

Google may derive approximate location from your connection and process analytics information outside New Zealand. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our website code. Browser and cookie identifiers can still distinguish visits; these statistics are not a count of everyone using the app.

The website also uses local storage for class data, settings and your analytics choice. Clearing browser storage removes your saved choice. Existing analytics cookies can be removed using your browser’s cookie controls.

Reviews

Public ratings and comments

The homepage review form sends your star rating, chosen display name, optional country and comment to Supabase for storage and moderation. A display name is optional; if you leave it blank, the review is attributed to “Teacher”. Country is chosen by you; this form does not track your location. No email address is requested, and class records are not attached.

Reviews are private until approved by TNT Teaching Tools. Submitting the form gives permission to publish the rating, display name, country (if provided) and comment. Published reviews are visible to anyone visiting the website. They are a moderated selection, not an unfiltered survey of all users.

Please do not include student details or private contact information. Pending reviews are retained for moderation and published reviews remain until removed. To ask for a correction or removal, contact TNT Teaching Tools using the email below and identify the review.

Supabase also processes technical information needed to operate the service and may process data outside New Zealand. Supabase privacy policy

06

Questions, access and corrections

Cold Call Classroom is operated by TNT Teaching Tools. For a privacy question, concern, or a request to access, correct or delete feedback information held about you, email:

tntteachingandlearning@gmail.com

We may need to confirm your identity before responding. TNT Teaching Tools cannot access class records stored only on your device. Teachers should handle requests about student records through their school’s normal process.
