Whiteboard recall

Make everyone’s thinking visible at the same time.

How to run it

  1. Give a cue

    Pose a prompt about previously taught knowledge.

  2. Think and write

    Allow independent thinking; everyone commits an answer.

  3. Reveal together

    Keep boards hidden until your signal, then scan the whole room.

  4. Correct and recheck

    Explain a common gap; students improve and answer a short follow-up.

Check learning

Sample across the room, not only the quickest responders. Avoid revealing another student’s board before everyone attempts.
