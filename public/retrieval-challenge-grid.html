Retrieval challenge grid

Revisit learning from different points in the course.

How to run it

  1. Prepare the grid

    Include prompts from last lesson, last week and earlier learning.

  2. Choose coverage

    Require at least one response from each time category.

  3. Retrieve

    Everyone answers independently before opening notes.

  4. Check gaps

    Review answers and correct missing or inaccurate knowledge.

Check learning

Age of content is not a reliable measure of difficulty. Points should never replace checking understanding.
